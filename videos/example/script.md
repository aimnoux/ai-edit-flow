# Example script

How a script is written for this kit. Speech is in `### N.M` blocks: `N` is the chapter, `M` the block.
A blank line between paragraphs is a pause. A word in [square brackets] is a trigger: an animation
starts on it, the word is read normally. Lines starting with `>` are notes for the editor, not speech.
Chapter titles are the `## N. Title` headings. `videos/example/video.json` sets the silence before the
first word of a chapter (title) and after the last one.

## 1. The question

### 1.1

Every language has a word for [hello]. Some have [three].

> On screen: the word "hello" large, then three variants appear below it.

### 1.2

Which one you pick says who you think you are [talking] to.

## 2. The answer

### 2.1

Formal when the person is a [stranger]. Short when they are a [friend]. And one more, for the people
you see [every] day.

### 2.2

That is the whole [trick]: the word is the same, the distance is not.
